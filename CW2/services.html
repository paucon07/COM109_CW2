01 — Spa & Thermal Pools

Heal in the warmth
of the earth

Drawn from 400 metres below the forest floor, our geothermal mineral pools carry a rich blend of magnesium, silica, and potassium — each one a natural remedy for tired muscles and an overworked mind. Step beneath the open sky and let the landscape do its ancient work.

Our spa complex houses five distinct thermal pools ranging from 36°C to 42°C, alongside cold plunge baths, a Finnish sauna, and a hand-built steam room lined with local cedar. Private pool alcoves can be reserved for couples or small groups seeking absolute seclusion.

02 — Wellness Gym

Strength forged
in the wild

Our open-air and glass-walled gymnasium sits at the edge of the old-growth forest, offering a training environment unlike any other. Floor-to-ceiling Douglas fir frames panoramic views of the valley below — a constant reminder of our connection to the natural world.

Designed with intention rather than excess, the gym pairs premium free weights with a dedicated movement studio for yoga, Pilates, and breathwork. Personal coaching sessions with our resident wellness practitioners are available by arrangement.
